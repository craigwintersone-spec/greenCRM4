Add the activities you run. Each one comes pre-set with stages, outcomes and item types. Change anything to match how you work, or build your own.
Set up each activity
Stages are the steps an item moves through. Outcomes are how it leaves. Item types fill in weight, CO₂e and value automatically when logged. Links pass items between activities.
Weights, CO₂e and £ values are Vorlana starter estimates. Replace them with your own or your funder's preferred figures. Reports always show the source.
