Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026
In short

Vorlana is run by Winters Ventures Ltd. When a charity or community organisation uses Vorlana to record its participants, volunteers and feedback, that organisation is in charge of that information and we look after it on their behalf. For our own website, customer accounts and enquiries, we are in charge. We never sell personal data and never use it for advertising.

2. Two different roles

When we are the controller

We decide how and why personal data is used for: visitors to vorlana.com; people who ask for a demo or contact us; the people who hold accounts on Vorlana (for example staff at a customer organisation); and our own business records such as invoices.

When we are the processor

Our customers use Vorlana to record information about the people they work with — participants, volunteers, event attendees, donors, partners and others. We call this Customer Data. The customer organisation is the controller of Customer Data and decides what is collected and why. We process it only on their instructions, under our Data processing agreement.

If you are a participant, volunteer or attendee of an organisation that uses Vorlana and you have a question about your information — including a request to see, correct or delete it — please contact that organisation directly. If you contact us, we will pass your request to them.

4. Customer Data

Customer Data can include names and contact details, attendance, volunteer hours, feedback, outcomes, notes, and — where a customer chooses to collect it — equality information such as age, ethnicity, gender, disability, religion or sexual orientation. Some of this is special category data under UK GDPR. Each customer is responsible for having a lawful basis, and where needed a condition under Article 9, for what it records.

Vorlana is designed to help customers collect less: feedback can be anonymous; equality questions are optional and default to “Prefer not to say”; postcodes on public forms are limited to the first half (for example PE2); and demographic answers from feedback forms are stored without a name or email attached.

We do not use Customer Data for our own purposes, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

5. AI features

Some features use AI provided by Anthropic to draft text such as reports and summaries. When you use one, we send Anthropic the information needed to produce that text — mainly figures Vorlana has already calculated and short excerpts such as feedback comments — and not whole records where it can be avoided. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its models by default. AI-generated text can contain mistakes, so customers should review it before sharing it.

6. Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted providers to run Vorlana. They only process data on our instructions. The current list, with locations, is on our Sub-processors page. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our customers, the public or ourselves.

7. International transfers

Some of our providers are based in, or may access data from, the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, we rely on adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with the provider's security measures.

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and demo requests — up to 2 years after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Account users — while the customer's account is active, then deleted as below.
  • Billing records — 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required by UK law.
  • Server logs — kept by our hosting providers for short periods, typically days to weeks.
  • Customer Data — until the customer deletes it or closes their account. After closure we delete it within 90 days, having given the customer the chance to export it. Copies in backups are overwritten as backups expire, within 30 days after that.

9. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, each organisation's data is kept separate at database level, and access by our own team is limited to what is needed to run and support the service. Details are on our Security & backups page.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: be told how your data is used; get a copy of it; have it corrected; have it deleted; restrict or object to how it is used; and, in some cases, move it to another provider. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To use these rights, email hello@vorlana.com. We will reply within one month. If your request is about Customer Data, we will pass it to the organisation responsible and help them respond.

11. Complaints

Please tell us first and we will try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
